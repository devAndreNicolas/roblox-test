# Bingo Match Playtest Plan

**Purpose:** Learn whether new players understand the loop and choose to play another round. This is a small usability and replay test, not proof of product-market fit.
**Behavior source:** [Feature specification](spec.md)

## Test setup

- Invite 3–8 friends to a private or coordinated session.
- Include desktop and at least one phone or tablet playthrough.
- Aim for 10–15 minutes and at least two rounds.
- Use the one-player Studio override only for functional checks; solo play cannot answer the social replay question.
- At the start, avoid explaining the rules beyond what the game itself shows.

## Observe

For each player, note:

- Whether they enter the queue without help.
- Whether they can explain the goal and marking action in their own words.
- Time until their first valid mark and moments of hesitation or confusion.
- Whether they stay through a round and choose to play another.
- Whether call pacing gives enough time to read, find, and mark.

Ask after play:

1. What were you trying to do during the round?
2. What part felt confusing or too slow?
3. Would you play one more round now? Why?

Record observations and direct quotes in [research/playtest-feedback.md](research/playtest-feedback.md), keeping raw feedback separate from the product interpretation.

## Decision after the session

- If players cannot explain the goal or mark without help, simplify the instruction and first-call flow.
- If players understand the game but miss calls, adjust the call interval or visual emphasis.
- If the group finishes but declines another round, revisit round length and the core loop before adding progression.
- If players replay but repeatedly struggle with controls, fix card readability and input first.

Use this small cohort to choose the next iteration, not to claim market validation.
